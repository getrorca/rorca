## What Makes Team Tier Worth It

The team tier is not a feature gate — it's a fundamentally different mode of operation:

**Shared direction stack**: When your lead engineer defines L1 (engineering principles) and L2 (domain conventions) once, every spec generated by every team member inherits those standards automatically. Without team tiers, each developer maintains their own direction stack — conventions drift, patterns diverge.

**Collaborative spec review**: Specs become team artifacts, not individual notebooks. Reviewers can approve, reject, or annotate stories before execution. The adversarial review pipeline runs team-level personas.

**Shared run history**: Anyone on the team can see what ran, what passed, what failed, and why. The episodic MEMORY.md ledger grows from team experience, not just individual experience.

**Role-based access**: Admins control what can be executed and approved. Editors write briefs and review specs. Viewers can observe runs without triggering them. Essential for orgs with junior devs running autonomous pipelines.

## Self-Hosted Note

Self-hosted users can run a multi-user instance themselves — but "multi-user" in self-hosted means they manage their own auth, their own DB, their own infra. The team features above (shared workspaces, RBAC, team direction) are built and tested in cloud but work in self-hosted too. Self-hosting is the technical option for orgs who want full data control.
